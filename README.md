# Permit Correction Helper: AI for Housing permit and process delays

Client: City of San José, City Housing
Team: Frank Leamy, Gabrielle Yang, Jet Lau, Anthony Trujillo, Matthew Throne, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I contributed to the project's research and analysis and built the login and dashboard pages for the staff and user, and helped with the implementation of the database.

## The problem
People and/or developers applying for building permits in San Jose can have difficulty in understanding correction letters because they contain technical planning and building terminology. Making it difficult for applicants like Alex, one of our personas, to understand what needs to be fixe before their application can move forward.

## What we built
-Our proposed AI prototype helps applicants understand correction letters by identifying required changes and showing what needs improvement in a simple manner
-The applicant uploads a permit letter or enters a questions, and the AI provides an explanation of the corrections and information needed
-A designated city planning reviewer must verify the AI-generated info before it is treated as official guidance. The AI does not approve permits or make final planning decisions

## What I would do next
One gap I found is that the AI has a possibility to provide incorrect or confusing explanations of permit correction letters, which could cause misinterpretations for what they need to fix. Next, I would test the AI with different correction letters to improve its accuracy and make sure the information is clear and reliable before a city planner in the city approves it.
