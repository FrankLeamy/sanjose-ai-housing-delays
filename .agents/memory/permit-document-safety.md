---
name: Permit document conflict and safety handling
description: Conflicting measurements and safety-related information must be escalated for human review.
---

When uploaded permit documents contain conflicting measurements or safety-related
information, clearly identify the evidence and recommend human review, not just
routine correction feedback. Do not choose a disputed measurement, declare safety,
or imply permit approval.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this safeguard for uploaded documents.

**How to apply:** Keep conflicts and safety warnings visible in saved and printed
reports. Incomplete extraction or unreadable drawings must be disclosed, not
interpreted as an absence of conflicts. A checklist checkbox is not professional
verification and must not dismiss a human-review warning.
