---
name: Legacy permitting question ownership
description: Why questions created before accounts should remain unattributed.
---

Questions saved before account support have no verified owner. Keep them unavailable in account-scoped history rather than assigning them to the first or newest user.

**Why:** There is no reliable way to establish who asked those questions; assigning them without evidence could expose someone else's questions and answers.

**How to apply:** When changing history or running a data backfill, leave unattributed questions unclaimed unless an explicit, verifiable ownership process is introduced.
